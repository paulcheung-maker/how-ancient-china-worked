## The short answer

Early Chinese direction-finding devices used the properties of lodestone and magnetized needles. Some early instruments were associated with divination and orientation; by the Song period, magnetized needles were used in navigation.

## From direction to navigation

A compass did not replace sailors' knowledge of stars, winds, currents, coastlines, and seasons. It added a dependable directional aid, especially when visibility was poor or routes extended farther from shore.

## Why the history is gradual

The “compass” was not born fully formed. Materials, mounting methods, and practical uses changed over centuries. Its importance lies in how craftsmen and sailors adapted magnetism to real travel.
