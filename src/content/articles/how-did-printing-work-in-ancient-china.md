## The short answer

Woodblock printing used a reversed text carved into a block, inked, and pressed onto paper. It was effective for reproducing many copies of the same page. Movable type was also developed, but woodblocks remained useful for Chinese texts with many characters.

## Printing changed circulation

Printing helped spread Buddhist texts, classics, calendars, medical works, examination guides, and popular literature. It lowered the cost of copying but did not make books equally available to everyone.

## More than a “printing press”

The familiar European press is not the only model for printing. Chinese book culture relied on paper, carving, skilled editors, publishers, merchants, readers, and schools. Technology worked because a whole system supported it.
