## The short answer

Gunpowder emerged from experiments with saltpeter, sulfur, and charcoal. Early formulas were used in incendiary devices and fireworks; later technologies included bombs, fire lances, rockets, and guns.

## A gradual change

Gunpowder did not immediately create modern firearms. Different mixtures, containers, projectiles, and military tactics developed over time. Chinese, Central Asian, Islamic, and European innovations all formed part of the later history of gunpowder weapons.

## Beyond warfare

Gunpowder also became associated with spectacle and ritual through fireworks. Its history shows how one material can move between medicine, alchemy, military technology, and celebration.
