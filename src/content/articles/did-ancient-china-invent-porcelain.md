## The short answer

China developed the high-fired ceramic traditions that produced what many historians call true porcelain. The process depended on particular clays, glazes, high temperatures, kilns, and skilled craftspeople; it developed over long periods rather than appearing at one moment.

## Why definitions matter

“Porcelain” is sometimes used loosely for fine white ceramics. Historians distinguish it from earlier pottery and stoneware by material and firing properties. Different regions developed distinct ceramic traditions and technologies.

## A global good

Ceramics were useful household objects and valuable trade goods. Chinese wares traveled through overland and maritime networks, while foreign demand and imported styles also influenced production.
